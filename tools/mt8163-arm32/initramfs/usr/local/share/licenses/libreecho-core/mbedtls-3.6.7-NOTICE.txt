The image rebuilds static ARM32 libmbedtls/libmbedx509/libmbedcrypto archives
from that pinned archive; the source offer records the compiler, the pinned
build requirements, and the SHA-256 of each produced archive
(tools/mt8163-arm32/mbedtls/SOURCE.lock and the builder's mbedtls-source.json).
The archives are statically linked into libreecho-web (HTTPS on port 8443) and
libreecho-radiod (HTTPS radio streams); no dynamic Mbed TLS library is shipped.
